=== ZynqMP 保护单元实配（经 EL3 只读窗口）===

[XPPU —— LPD 从机的许可表  base 0xFF980000]
  XPPU_CTRL              @0xff980000 = 0x00000000
  XPPU_ERR_STATUS1       @0xff980004 = 0x00000000
  XPPU_ERR_STATUS2       @0xff980008 = 0x00000000
  XPPU_POISON            @0xff98000c = 0x000ff9c0
  XPPU_ISR               @0xff980010 = 0x00000000
  XPPU_IMR               @0xff980014 = 0x000000ef
  XPPU_LOCK              @0xff98001c = 0x00000000

[XMPU_DDR0..5 状态]

  DDR1  CTRL=0000000b  ERR_ADDR=0006002e  ERR_MASTER=000000ac(APU)  POISON=00000000  ISR=00000008[SECURTYVIO]  LOCK=00000000
  DDR2  CTRL=0000000b  ERR_ADDR=00060039  ERR_MASTER=000000a4(APU)  POISON=00000000  ISR=00000008[SECURTYVIO]  LOCK=00000000
  DDR3  CTRL=0000000b  ERR_ADDR=00000000  ERR_MASTER=00000000(RPU0)  POISON=00000000  ISR=00000000  LOCK=00000000
  DDR4  CTRL=0000000b  ERR_ADDR=00000000  ERR_MASTER=00000000(RPU0)  POISON=00000000  ISR=00000000  LOCK=00000000
  DDR5  CTRL=0000000b  ERR_ADDR=00000000  ERR_MASTER=00000000(RPU0)  POISON=00000000  ISR=00000000  LOCK=00000000
