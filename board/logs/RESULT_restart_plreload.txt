########## PL 重配当 restart（200 行 × 1000 样本，表征形态位流 9322b14b）

=== 采集端写进文件头的口径 ===
  #SP800-90B-restart-matrix rows=200 cols=1000
  #restart=PL-RECONFIGURATION (whole fabric reprogrammed; the ring
  # oscillator is re-instantiated and every flop returns to its
  # bitstream init value. Closer to power-on than a TRNG zeroize, but
  # still NOT a power-on reset: the chip stayed powered, the supplies
  # were not re-established, and the die temperature did not return to
  # ambient.)
  #tap=RAW (pre-conditioning, src_valid/src_bit)
  #sampling=AFTER startup_done AND draining 128 raw words (operational)
  #rows=200 is BELOW the 1000 that SP 800-90B suggests; the per-column
  # estimate therefore has less statistical power. Do not report this as
  # a 1000-row result.
  #binary payload follows: rows x ceil(cols/8) bytes, MSB-first within each byte

=== 重启矩阵 200 x 1000 ===
采集端写进文件头的口径：

按行（一次重启之内）  最小 0.776956  中位 0.860558  最大 0.886972

H_restart = min(行, 列) = 0.506660 比特/样本
H_原始（顺序采集）      = 0.871234 比特/样本
判据：H_restart >= H_原始 / 2 = 0.435617
→ **通过**
